## Title
ALTERA · Y2K fashion web experience

## Description (paste)
ALTERA is a Y2K-inspired clothing brand. For Drop 001 "Red Signal" I created the art direction, the AI model and imagery, the identity, the posters and the launch website.

The site is a cinematic scroll story where scrolling is the camera. Visitors can build their alter ego in a styling studio of 9 pieces and 27 looks, rotate the model 360° by dragging, explore the pieces on a rotating showcase and join the waitlist. Pixel and Y2K energy, presented as a premium fashion label, not a game.

Live site: https://alterarelease.store
